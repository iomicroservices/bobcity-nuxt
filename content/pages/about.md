Bob City started with a simple idea: property owners should not need three different contractors for everyday jobs.

We combine handyman repairs, painting & decorating, and removals under one roof so projects stay coordinated and straightforward. Whether you need a same-week fix, a full redecorate, or help moving house, we aim for clear quotes, tidy sites, and work we are proud to put our name on.
