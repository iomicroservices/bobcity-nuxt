A little preparation before painting day makes a big difference. Clear surfaces, move lighter furniture to the centre of the room, and remove wall fixtures where practical.

Protect floors and soft furnishings, and point out any known damp patches or cracks so they can be treated properly. The better the prep, the cleaner and longer-lasting the finish.
